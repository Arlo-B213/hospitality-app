## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
- Internet connection (recommended; offline mode available)
- Mobile phone or tablet (or desktop)

### Accessing the App

1. Open your browser and navigate to `https://pride-training-app.com` (or provided URL)
2. You'll see the login screen

### Creating Your Account

**If you don't have an account yet:**

1. Click **"Sign Up"** on the login page
2. Enter your details:
   - Email address (must be unique)
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Phone (optional)
   - Password (must include: uppercase, lowercase, number, special character)
3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. You'll be logged in automatically and taken to the dashboard

**Note:** Self-registered users start as "New Hire" role. Managers can create team lead and manager accounts.

### Logging In

1. Enter your email address
2. Enter your password
3. Click **"Login"**
4. You'll be taken to your dashboard

**Forgot Your Password?**
- Contact your manager to reset it through the admin panel

## For Team Members (FOH/BOH Staff)

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see:
- **Your Info** — Your name and role
- **Your Team's New Hires** — List of people you're evaluating
- **Quick Stats** — Number of active hires, average progress

### Evaluating a New Hire (Step-by-Step)

#### 1. Find the New Hire

From the dashboard:
1. Look for the new hire's name in the list under **"Team Members to Evaluate"**
2. Click their name or the **"Evaluate"** button

#### 2. View Their Progress

You'll see:
- **Days Elapsed** — How many days into their 90-day period
- **Completion %** — Overall progress (based on feedback received)
- **Previous Ratings** — Skills already rated by you and others
- **Leadership Modules** — Training milestones (if applicable)

#### 3. Rate Their Skills

1. Scroll to the **"Add Evaluation"** section
2. Select a skill from the dropdown (e.g., "Order Accuracy," "Communication," "Teamwork")
3. Choose a rating on the 1-5 scale:
   - **1 — Needs Improvement:** Struggles with this skill; needs coaching
   - **2 — Developing:** Making progress but not consistent
   - **3 — Competent:** Can do the task independently
   - **4 — Proficient:** Does it well, helps others
   - **5 — Mastery:** Consistently excellent; sets the standard
4. Add optional notes (e.g., "Excellent communication with guests")
5. Click **"Save Rating"**

#### 4. Leave Notes/Feedback

1. In the **"Notes & Coaching"** section, click **"Add Note"**
2. Write specific feedback (examples: "Great with table management," "Still learning POS system")
3. Click **"Save Note"**

Notes are timestamped with your name and immediately visible to managers.

### Available Skills by Department

**FOH (Front of House):**
- Order Accuracy
- Guest Communication
- Table Management
- POS System Proficiency
- Payment Processing
- Upselling/Menu Knowledge

**BOH (Back of House):**
- Food Preparation Quality
- Kitchen Safety
- Speed & Efficiency
- Equipment Operation
- Hygiene Standards
- Teamwork/Communication

**Shared (All Departments):**
- Teamwork
- Communication
- Attitude/Reliability
- Problem-Solving
- Customer Focus

### Viewing Team Analytics

To see your full team's progress:
1. From the dashboard, click **"Team Analytics"** (if available to your role)
2. You'll see:
   - Average skill ratings by department
   - Individual new hire progress
   - Trends over time

## For Team Leads

Team leads have all the capabilities of team members, plus:

### Create New Hire Records

1. From the dashboard, click **"Add New Hire"**
2. Enter:
   - First and Last Name
   - Department (FOH or BOH)
   - Start Date (typically today)
   - Target Completion Date (90 days from start)
   - Optional notes
3. Click **"Create"**
4. The new hire appears in your list; you and your team can start evaluating

### Manage Your Team's Evaluations

1. Click any team member's name to see their evaluations
2. View all ratings from all evaluators (yourself, other leads, managers)
3. Add or update your own ratings and notes
4. See the automatically calculated progress percentage

### Export Progress Reports

1. Click **"Export Report"** on a new hire's profile
2. Choose format: PDF or CSV
3. Includes all ratings, notes, and analytics
4. Use for documentation or sharing with managers

### Leadership Module Tracking

1. On a new hire's profile, find the **"Leadership Development"** section
2. View the 5 Leadership Pillars:
   - Emotional Intelligence
   - Decisiveness
   - Delegation & Accountability
   - Coaching & Developing Others
   - Cross-Functional Communication
3. As the lead, mark modules complete when the new hire demonstrates mastery
4. Click **"Mark Complete"** with today's date

## For Managers

Managers have full visibility and control over team evaluations and analytics.

### Dashboard Features

From the manager dashboard, you see:
- **All Teams Overview** — FOH and BOH status
- **Key Metrics** — Average completion %, average skill ratings
- **New Hire Pipeline** — All active hires across both departments
- **Quick Actions** — Create user, view analytics, export reports

### Managing New Hires

#### View All New Hires
1. Click **"All New Hires"**
2. Filter by department (FOH/BOH) or search by name
3. Click a name to view their full profile

#### Create a New Hire
1. Click **"Add New Hire"**
2. Enter:
   - Name
   - Department (FOH or BOH)
   - Start Date
   - Target Completion Date (90 days default)
   - Notes
3. Click **"Create"**

#### Assign Team Leads to New Hires
1. On the new hire's profile, scroll to **"Assigned Evaluators"**
2. Click **"Add Evaluator"**
3. Select team member's name and confirm
4. They'll now see this hire in their list to evaluate

#### Mark Leadership Modules Complete
1. On the new hire's profile, go to **"Leadership Modules"**
2. Click **"Mark Complete"** next to each pillar as they master it
3. Timestamp is automatically recorded

#### End a New Hire's Probation (Mark as Inactive)
1. Click **"More Options"** on their profile
2. Click **"Mark Inactive"** or **"End Probation"**
3. Confirm
4. They'll no longer appear in active hire lists but their data is preserved

### Team Analytics & Reporting

#### View Cohort Summary
1. Click **"Analytics"** → **"Cohort Summary"**
2. Select department (FOH or BOH)
3. See:
   - List of all new hires in progress
   - Individual progress percentages
   - Average skill ratings
   - Leadership module completion
   - Comparison against department average

#### Individual New Hire Analytics
1. Click on any new hire's name from the analytics view
2. See detailed metrics:
   - Skill trends over time (line graph showing week-by-week ratings)
   - Skills by category (Technical vs. Soft Skills)
   - Leadership module timeline
   - Cohort comparison (how they compare to others)

#### Export Reports
1. From any new hire profile, click **"Export Report"**
2. Choose:
   - **PDF** — For formal documentation
   - **CSV** — For spreadsheet analysis
3. Report includes all ratings, notes, and calculated metrics

### Creating Team Member Accounts

1. Click **"User Management"** (admin panel)
2. Click **"Create New User"**
3. Enter:
   - Email
   - Full Name
   - Role (Lead, Staff, etc.)
   - Department (FOH or BOH)
   - Temporary Password
4. Click **"Create"**
5. Share the temporary password with the new user
6. They'll be prompted to change it on first login

### Viewing Audit Logs

For compliance and tracking, view activity logs:
1. Click **"Settings"** → **"Audit Logs"** (admin only)
2. Filter by:
   - Date range
   - User
   - Action type
3. See: Who did what, when, and from which IP address

## For Admins

Admins have complete control over the system, including user management, configuration, and audit trails.

### User Management

#### Create New Users
1. Go to **"User Management"** → **"Users"**
2. Click **"Add New User"**
3. Select role:
   - **Manager** — Full team oversight
   - **Assistant Manager** — Limited user creation
   - **Team Lead** — Can evaluate and assign leads
   - **Staff** — Can evaluate own team only
   - **New Hire** — Limited access
4. Assign department (FOH/BOH)
5. Set temporary password
6. Click **"Create"**

#### Modify User Roles & Permissions
1. Go to **"User Management"** → **"Users"**
2. Find and click the user
3. Edit role, department, or team assignment
4. Click **"Update"**

#### Reset User Password
1. Find the user in the list
2. Click **"More"** → **"Reset Password"**
3. Temporary password is generated and shown
4. Share with the user; they'll change it on login

#### Deactivate or Delete Users
1. Find the user
2. Click **"More"** → **"Deactivate"** (keeps data)
3. Or click **"Delete"** (removes account; data preserved)

### System Configuration

#### Security Settings
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Security"**
2. Configure:
   - Password requirements (min length, complexity)
   - Session timeout (auto-logout after inactivity)
   - Rate limiting (login attempt limits)
   - Two-factor authentication (if available)

#### Email & Notifications
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Notifications"**
2. Configure:
   - Email on new hire completion
   - Email on milestone reached
   - Digest frequency (daily/weekly)

#### System Maintenance
1. Go to **"Settings"** → **"Maintenance"**
2. Options:
   - **Database Backup** — Download backup file
   - **Export All Data** — CSV or JSON
   - **Clear Archived Data** — Remove old records (careful!)

### Audit & Compliance

#### View Audit Logs
1. Go to **"Audit Logs"**
2. See all system activity:
   - User logins and logouts
   - Data changes (ratings, hires)
   - Role and permission changes
   - Password resets
3. Filter by:
   - Date range
   - User
   - Action type
   - New hire

#### Generate Compliance Reports
1. Go to **"Reports"** → **"Compliance"**
2. Choose period and department
3. Report includes:
   - All evaluations conducted
   - Audit trail
   - Data integrity checks
4. Download as PDF for records

## Offline Usage

PRIDE Training App works offline using your browser's storage. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

### Offline Capabilities

**What works offline:**
- Viewing cached new hire data
- Creating local evaluations/ratings
- Typing notes and feedback
- Browsing your team list

**What requires internet:**
- Logging in (first time)
- Downloading new data
- Uploading changes to server
- Viewing analytics (may show cached data)

### Using Offline

1. **On poor connection:** The app automatically detects and switches to offline mode
2. **Intentional offline:** Use airplane mode or disable wifi
3. **Making changes:** Create evaluations as normal; they're stored locally
4. **Blue indicator:** Look for a **"Sync Pending"** indicator (usually top right)
5. **Reconnect:** When you regain internet, changes upload automatically
6. **Manual sync:** If needed, go to **Settings** → **"Sync Now"**

### Managing Offline Data

To free up storage on your device:
1. Go to **Settings** → **"Storage"**
2. See how much data is cached
3. Click **"Clear Cache"** (will redownload when needed)
4. Or **"Download Latest Data"** to refresh your offline copy

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In

**Problem:** Invalid email/password error
- **Solution:** Double-check email spelling and password (case-sensitive)
- **Solution:** Reset password by contacting your manager

**Problem:** Too many login attempts
- **Solution:** Wait 15 minutes; too many failed attempts trigger rate limiting
- **Solution:** Check your email for account recovery link (if available)

### Can't See a New Hire in My List

**Problem:** New hire not showing in my evaluations list
- **Causes:**
  - New hire is in a different department (FOH vs. BOH)
  - You're not assigned as an evaluator for them
  - New hire has been marked inactive
- **Solution:** Contact your manager to confirm assignment

### Rating Won't Save

**Problem:** "Save failed" error when rating a skill
- **Solution:** Check internet connection (if online required)
- **Solution:** Refresh the page and try again
- **Solution:** Try a different browser
- **Contact:** If persists, report to your manager

### Offline Mode Not Working

**Problem:** Can't work offline even though app was previously used
- **Causes:**
  - Browser storage was cleared
  - You've never accessed the data before
- **Solution:** Connect to internet and load the app once to cache data
- **Solution:** Go to **Settings** → **"Download for Offline"** to force caching

### Changes Not Syncing

**Problem:** Made changes offline but they're not appearing after going online
- **Solution:** Give it 30 seconds after reconnecting; sync happens automatically
- **Solution:** Go to **Settings** → **"Sync Now"** to trigger manual sync
- **Solution:** Refresh the page
- **Contact:** If data is lost, contact your admin

### Slow Performance / App Freezing

**Problem:** App is slow or freezing
- **Solution:** Clear browser cache (DevTools → Application → Clear Storage)
- **Solution:** Close other tabs/apps to free up memory
- **Solution:** Try incognito/private browsing mode
- **Solution:** Update your browser to latest version

### Can't Export Report

**Problem:** Export button is grayed out or not working
- **Causes:**
  - Only available for leads/managers
  - No data to export for new hire
- **Solution:** Ensure you're logged in with appropriate role
- **Solution:** Add at least one evaluation/rating before exporting
- **Contact:** Contact your manager if you need export capability

### Mobile App Not Installed Properly

**Problem:** App won't install on iOS/Android
- **Solution:** Open the URL in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android)
- **Solution:** Look for "Share" → "Add to Home Screen" (iOS) or "Install App" (Android)
- **Solution:** Ensure you have space available on device
- **Contact:** For persistent issues, email support

## Best Practices

### For Accurate Evaluations

1. **Rate Based on Observation** — Rate what you've actually seen them do
2. **Be Specific in Notes** — Instead of "Good job," say "Accurately processes orders 95% of the time"
3. **Regular Feedback** — Don't wait 90 days; evaluate throughout the period
4. **Consistency** — Use the 1-5 scale consistently (don't mark everyone 3s)
5. **Constructive Coaching** — Use notes to provide actionable feedback

### For Managers

1. **Review Weekly** — Check new hire progress at least weekly
2. **Share Feedback** — Discuss ratings with leads to ensure fairness
3. **Celebrate Progress** — Highlight improvements and growth
4. **Address Issues Early** — If someone is struggling, discuss with leads
5. **Document Everything** — Keep audit logs; they protect you legally

### For Data Privacy

1. **Don't Share Passwords** — Each person gets their own login
2. **Log Out When Done** — Don't leave session open on shared devices
3. **Limit Access** — Managers/admins see all data; staff see only their team
4. **Offline Data** — Only on your device; not backed up to cloud
